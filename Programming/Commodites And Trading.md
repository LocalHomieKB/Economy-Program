For anything related to variable subheadings, for example, COMMODITY followed with a ( _ ) plus whatever name is appropriate. So COMMODITY_LIST would be the list of commodities. COMMODITY_LIST_PRICE would therefore be the prices of the commodity list

COMMODITY - Variables related to commodities
LIST - list of commodities
PRICE - list of Price of each commodity 

NEIGHBOUR will be the next variable subheading
TOMB - Tombstone town variables
DENIS - St Denis town variables
ARMA - Armadillo town variables
POPULATION - Population of the town
BUILDINGS - Important building of the town
PRODUCTION - Commodities produced
DEMAND - Commodities in demand
